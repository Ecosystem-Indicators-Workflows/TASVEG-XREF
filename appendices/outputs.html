17  Overview of workflow outputs

To preserve a clean separation between analytical documentation (this Quarto book in docs-src/) and data-product generation, all computationally intensive spatial outputs are produced by standalone, reproducible R scripts stored in scripts/ and written to dedicated subfolders under results/. Each output package is documented with Frictionless Data Package schemas (datapackage.json), Zenodo deposit metadata (.zenodo.json), and a dynamically rendered README.qmd before deposition on Zenodo.

Figure 17.1 illustrates how TASVEG 5.0 spatial polygons and multi-interpreter membership tables feed into the three core state-level outputs.

## 1. Overview & Methodology

This dataset provides **`r n_efgs` spatial raster layers (`r res_xy[1]` m resolution GeoTIFFs)** representing the aggregated fuzzy membership (`prop_memb`) of Tasmania's statewide vegetation map (**TASVEG 5.0**) to each candidate Level 3 **Ecosystem Functional Group (EFG)** of the **IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (IUCN GET)**.

### Why Per-EFG Membership Rasters?

When cross-referencing regional vegetation classifications (TASVEG 5.0) to global functional typologies (IUCN GET), individual mapping units (`VEGCODE`) frequently have partial or one-to-many relationships across multiple target EFGs (due to environmental gradients, broad mapping complex definitions, or expert uncertainty).

- Whereas a single **"best-candidate" ("winner-takes-all")** map retains only the single highest-scoring EFG per source unit (`r n_best_efgs` EFGs in Tasmania), this **per-EFG membership raster stack** retains **all `r n_efgs` candidate EFGs** assigned any non-zero membership in Round `r round_nr` across the `r n_interp` interpreters—including **`r n_nonbest_efgs` additional EFGs** that represent secondary/partial ecosystem components within TASVEG mapping units.
- For each source `VEGCODE` $s$ and candidate target EFG $t$, the aggregated membership proportion is calculated across all `r n_interp` interpreters in Round `r round_nr` (`ecoSynthesis::summarise_membership(..., keep_all = TRUE, which_round = 2)`):
  $$\text{prop\_memb}_{s, t} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{`r n_interp`} m_{s, t, i}}{\sum_{k} \sum_{i=1}^{`r n_interp`} m_{s, k, i}}$$
- For each EFG $t$, polygons in `TASVEG_5_0.shp` with $\text{prop\_memb}_{s, t} > 0$ are rasterized at **`r res_xy[1]` m × `r res_xy[2]` m** resolution using `fasterize::fasterize(..., field = "prop_memb", fun = "sum")`. Pixels outside polygons assigned to EFG $t$ (as well as the three non-eligible natural units `OAQ`, `ORO`, and `OSM`) are encoded as `NoData` (`NA`).

## 1. Overview & Methodology

This dataset provides a **`r res_xy[1]` m resolution spatial synthesis** and **tabular cross-reference lookup tables** aligning Tasmania's statewide vegetation map (**TASVEG 5.0**) with Level 3 **Ecosystem Functional Groups (EFGs)** of the **IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (IUCN GET)**.

### Cross-Referencing & Expert Elicitation Workflow

Following the IUCN standards and guidelines for cross-referencing ecosystem classifications and maps (IUCN, 2025), the workflow proceeded through four stages:

1. **Conceptual Alignment & Eligibility Assessment:**
   - **Source classification:** TASVEG 5.0 (`LIST_TASVEG_50_STATEWIDE`, released November 2025; Kitchener & Harris, 2013), comprising **`r n_total_units` unique mapping units (`VEGCODE`)** in the statewide polygon shapefile across mainland Tasmania and surrounding islands.
   - **Eligibility:** **`r n_eligible_units` mapping units** represent ecological vegetation communities or anthropogenic/modified ecosystems eligible for cross-referencing to the IUCN GET. **`r n_ineligible` mapping units** in the group *Other natural environments* (`r ineligible_list`) represent broad mapping complexes combining multiple distinct ecosystem types or non-vegetated substrates that cannot be unambiguously aligned to a single ecosystem concept without auxiliary spatial data; these `r n_ineligible` units are flagged as `eligible = FALSE` and assigned `NoData` (`NA`) in the spatial rasters. *(Note: Three additional codes present in the TASVEG 5.0 Fire Attributes DBF lookup table—`FPL`, `HUE`, and `SLW`—do not occur as polygons in the statewide shapefile).*
   - **Target classification:** IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (Keith et al., 2022) at **Level 3: Ecosystem Functional Groups (EFGs)**.

2. **Two-Round Structured Expert Elicitation:**
   - `r n_interp` domain experts (MGT, DAK, AL, AK, JB) independently assessed the fuzzy membership ($0 < \text{memb} \le 1$, summing to $1.0$ per source unit and interpreter) of each eligible TASVEG 5.0 community across candidate IUCN GET EFGs.
   - Discrepancies and contested units from **Round 1** were systematically reviewed in group discussions, after which experts provided revised assessments in **Round `r round_nr`**.

3. **Synthesis ("Best Candidate" / Winner-Takes-All):**
   - Using the validated **Round `r round_nr`** assessments across all **`r n_interp` interpreters**, membership scores were aggregated by `VEGCODE` using `ecoSynthesis::summarise_membership(..., keep_all = FALSE, which_round = 2)`.
   - For each source `VEGCODE`, the candidate EFG receiving the highest **proportion of total membership votes (`prop_memb`)** across the `r n_interp` interpreters was selected as the **best candidate**.
   - Across the `r n_eligible_units` eligible TASVEG 5.0 units, **`r n_efgs` IUCN GET Ecosystem Functional Groups** were selected as best candidates, with `prop_memb` ranging from **`r sprintf("%.2f", prop_min)` to `r sprintf("%.2f", prop_max)`** (median **`r sprintf("%.2f", prop_median)`**, mean **`r sprintf("%.3f", prop_mean)`**). For **`r supp_max_row$n_units` of `r n_eligible_units` units** (`r sprintf("%.1f%%", supp_max_row$pct_area)` of mapped area), all `r supp_max_row$supporting` interpreters assigned non-zero membership to the winning EFG (`supporting = ` `r supp_max_row$supporting`).

4. **Spatial Rasterization (`r res_xy[1]` m GeoTIFFs):**
   - The synthesised table was joined to the TASVEG 5.0 polygon geometries (`TASVEG_5_0.shp`) and rasterized at **`r res_xy[1]` m × `r res_xy[2]` m resolution** in ``r crs_label`` using `fasterize::fasterize()`.
   - Standard integer pixel codes (`efg_pixel_value`) and official hex color tables (`efg_hex_code`) from the Global Ecosystems Atlas (`geco` R package) were embedded into the target GeoTIFF.

### Summary of Uncertainty Layers

- **`TASVEG-xref-membership.tiff` (`prop_memb`)**:
  - Represents the proportion of total membership votes across all `r n_interp` interpreters in Round `r round_nr` assigned to the winning EFG:
    $$\text{prop\_memb}_{s, t^*} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{`r n_interp`} m_{s, t^*, i}}{\sum_{t} \sum_{i=1}^{`r n_interp`} m_{s, t, i}}$$
  - Values range from **`r sprintf("%.2f", memb_minmax[1])`** (contested/broadly split source unit) to **`r sprintf("%.2f", memb_minmax[2])`** (unanimous 100% assignment by all `r n_interp` interpreters).
- **`TASVEG-xref-supporting.tiff` (`supporting`)**:
  - Count of interpreters (out of `r n_interp` in Round `r round_nr`) who assigned $m_{s, t^*, i} > 0$ to the best-candidate EFG:

## 1. Overview & Methodology

This dataset provides a **polygon vector layer (`TASVEG-xref-TF1.7-memb.gpkg`)** and **unit-level summary table (`TASVEG-xref-TF1.7-unit-summary.csv`)** for **Level 3 Ecosystem Functional Group `TF1.7` (*Boreal and temperate fens*)** of the **IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology (IUCN GET)** in Tasmania, derived from **TASVEG 5.0**.

### Selection & Cross-Referencing Methodology

1. **Candidate Unit Filtering:**
   - Out of the 153 eligible vegetation community mapping units in **TASVEG 5.0**, **``r n_units`` vegetation communities (`VEGCODE`)** were assigned non-zero fuzzy membership ($\text{memb} > 0$) to **`TF1.7` (*Boreal and temperate fens*)** by at least one of the ``r n_interp`` domain experts in **Round ``r round_nr``** of structured expert elicitation.
   - All **``r format(n_polygons, big.mark = ",")`` polygons** belonging to these ``r n_units`` `VEGCODE` units were extracted from `TASVEG_5_0.shp` (total unweighted polygon area: **``r format(round(total_unweighted_km2, 2), big.mark = ",", nsmall = 2)`` $\text{km}^2$**).

2. **Individual Interpreter & Aggregated Membership Attributes:**
   - To preserve full transparency across interpreters, the polygon attribute table includes both:
     - **Individual interpreter fuzzy memberships** in Round ``r round_nr`` (`memb_AK`, `memb_AL`, `memb_DAK`, `memb_JB`, `memb_MGT`, each in $[0, 1]$), and
     - **Aggregated synthesis metrics** across all ``r n_interp`` interpreters:
       - `prop_memb`: Proportion of total Round ``r round_nr`` membership votes assigned to `TF1.7` ($\sum_{i=1}^{`r n_interp`} m_{s, \text{TF1.7}, i} / `r n_interp`$).
       - `supporting`: Number of interpreters (out of ``r n_interp``) assigning $>0$ membership to `TF1.7`.
       - `best`: Logical flag (`TRUE` / `FALSE`) indicating whether `TF1.7` is the single highest-scoring ("best") candidate EFG for that `VEGCODE` (`TRUE` for ``r best_unit_code``; `FALSE` for the remaining ``r n_units - n_best_units`` units where `TF1.7` represents a partial/secondary ecosystem component).
